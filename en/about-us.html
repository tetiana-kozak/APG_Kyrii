Who we are

ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN GROUP
OF ANNA KYRII

For 18 years on the market, we have been creating projects that contribute to the development of Ukraine.

Our mission is to create modern architecture for ambitious projects based on highly qualified design documentation, which we develop with high quality and deliver on time, with transparent management using BIM technologies.

The company’s office is located in Kyiv.
