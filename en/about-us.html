Why us

SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT AND EXPERIENCE

18 years on the market, 290 completed projects, 96 clients satisfied with the results, and implemented landmark projects demonstrate our experience and consistent growth

FROM THE FIRST IDEA TO THE RIBBON CUTTING

We provide consultation at every stage and take responsibility for the entire project cycle — from the initial idea to the completed building. We see the big picture while paying attention to detail, ensuring that every element aligns with the overall concept in the conceptual design, working documentation, and construction

GENERAL DESIGN (LEAD DESIGNER ROLE)

We act as the Lead Designer, taking responsibility for all related engineering disciplines — their quality, scope, coordination, and final outcome. We ensure proper management and consistency of solutions

BIM-BASED DESIGN

In our projects, drawings correspond to the scope of work, and work volumes match the cost estimates thanks to the use of BIM technologies. This enables the client to easily control both the construction process and their investments

WE GUARANTEE POSITIVE EXPERT REVIEW RESULTS

Our specialists have in-depth knowledge of legislation and building codes and stay up to date with all changes. We develop projects with high quality and know how to implement solutions in full compliance with regulations, ensuring consistently positive expert reviews

PERMITS AND CONSTRUCTION WITHOUT CORRUPTION

We do not tolerate bribes or kickbacks. Through thorough analysis of input data, risk management, and a clear understanding of all procedures, our clients obtain permits exclusively through legal means. When selecting materials and equipment, we are guided solely by the efficiency of design solutions for the client. No one in our company receives kickbacks from manufacturers

A PROFESSIONAL TEAM WITH STRONG EXPERTISE

We carefully build our team and select partners, accumulating the company’s institutional experience and strengthening it with the competencies of our specialists

RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE RESULT

We take responsibility for every design decision thanks to our compact team and well-coordinated processes. We are passionate about architecture and committed to delivering our projects. We maintain strict deadline discipline and always achieve results together with the client

EXPERIENCED AND TRANSPARENT MANAGEMENT

We are experienced in working on projects with multiple stakeholders and parallel processes. Our clients regularly receive reports and updates on project progress. No question or message is left without a qualified response — we provide full project support

RISK MANAGEMENT

Thorough analysis of input data and client requirements, along with control of processes and decisions at every stage, allows us to effectively manage project risks

WE LOVE COMPLEX TASKS AND MULTI-LEVEL PROJECTS

We do not design private houses or apartment interiors. Our specialists focus on complex, system-based architectural challenges for commercial clients and Ukrainian communities. We understand all project stakeholders and know how to balance their interests

IF THERE IS A CHALLENGE — WE WILL FIND A SOLUTION

In our design approach, we follow the principles of modern architecture and always find the best architectural and engineering solutions for our clients’ needs

Your way

1

First, we listen to and understand your request to define the key objectives of the project

2

Next, we analyze the site and property documentation, as well as applicable regulations and laws, before providing a commercial proposal (CP) with timelines and a project roadmap

3

We agree on the CP, sign the contract, and receive the advance payment

4

We develop concept solutions to approve the design brief and form a shared vision of the project

5

Only after that do we begin developing pre-design proposals, ensuring you receive exactly what you envisioned and planned

6

We always provide pre-design proposals with visualizations to help you understand the final appearance. Upon request, we can also create a 3D model fly-through video

7

Before starting the “Project” stage, we provide a list of required input data and inform you about potential project risks

8

We develop the “Project” stage with high quality, attention to detail, within clearly defined timelines, and with cost estimate documentation to minimize possible changes during the “Working Documentation” stage and construction

9

We produce working documentation in a BIM model, allowing us to provide builders not only with precise specifications but also a 3D model for visualizing required elements and details

10

It is important for us to bring both our ideas and yours to life, so we carry out regular author supervision, resolve all ongoing issues, and ensure that the completed building aligns with the overall concept
