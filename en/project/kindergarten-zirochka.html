“ZIRORKA” PRESCHOOL

Address: Brovary, Kyiv Oblast, 3 Yaroslav Mudryi St.
Client: Capital Construction Department of the Brovary City Council
Team: Anna Kyriy, Daria Yurkevych, Stanislav Bagriy, Kateryna Shynkarenko, Hanna Shkolna
Status: Design and construction phases with all sections of the turnkey project, including cost estimates, expert review, and design supervision. The facility is currently under construction
Area: 1 850 m²
Year: 2026
CHALLENGE

The preschool group of the existing kindergarten was located in an old, dilapidated building that did not meet modern standards. The challenge was to fit a new, modern building, all the playgrounds, and shaded canopies for different age groups onto a compact site, while integrating the two buildings into a single complex through spatial design solutions

SOLUTION

The new building is positioned on the site to create a cozy courtyard for children in conjunction with the old building. The main entrances to the buildings face each other. All necessary service entrances are located on the exterior for the convenience and safety of the children. The main highlight of the spatial design is the integration of shade canopies into the building’s architectural composition.

Thanks to the varied window configurations, we ensured maximum natural light in all rooms of the kindergarten. The lobby at the main entrance is designed as a high, two-story space with a gallery and overhead lighting through a skylight. The auditorium opens onto an accessible roof, from which stairs lead down to the courtyard. The kindergarten is inclusive, with learning opportunities and accessibility to all rooms for children and adults with special needs.

FEATURES

We want to incorporate art into the children’s space. The central pillar in the lobby will be adorned with an original mosaic panel titled “Starry Sky” by the renowned artist Anna Movenko
